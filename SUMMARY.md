# Orbital Deployment Trade Study: one page

The package answers a bounded preliminary question: what does a tangential deployment impulse
do to a circular orbit, how quickly does mean-motion drift accumulate phase, what recoil does the
host receive, and what ideal rigid-body motion follows an internal mass transfer?

The corrected internal-mass analysis distinguishes peak rate from residual rate. A symmetric
closed move ends with zero ideal residual body rate but leaves an attitude offset. Flexible modes,
damping, controller behavior and restoration time remain open.

The retained record keeps the A5 FAIL, A6 VOID rows and unrun A9 visible. The package is a trade
calculator, not a conjunction product or flight-dynamics certification.
